Searches OpenStreetMap's free public business directory — real company names, addresses, phone, email, and website where mapped. Matching favors precision: niche verticals (TRT, med spa, personal injury) match on business name/category rather than a generic OSM tag, so you get fewer, more relevant results instead of every clinic or lawyer in the radius. Coverage varies by area and won't match Google Maps' completeness, and there's no review count, ad status, or SEO data — you'll still classify those signals with the dropdowns below. Capped at ~120 results per search — narrow the radius or keyword in dense metros to see closer matches. Results are added to your current list and deduplicated automatically.
Recognizes common headers automatically: Company/Business Name, First/Last Name or Owner, Phone, Email, Location/Address, Website, LinkedIn, Reviews, Rating. Unrecognized columns are ignored. Duplicate company names are skipped.
